Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

This website has no contact forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. If you ring or email us, we hold what you share with us (such as your name, contact details and your child's details) only for handling your enquiry and any enrolment that follows.

How we use it

Information you give us is used to respond to you, arrange visits, and manage enrolments. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and the requirements that apply to licensed early childhood services.

Children's information

Records about enrolled tamariki are collected and stored under our centre policies and Ministry of Education requirements. They are shared only where the law requires or with your consent.

Third-party services

This site embeds Google Maps and links to social media platforms. Those services may set their own cookies and collect usage data under their own privacy policies. We do not run our own analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see or correct personal information we hold about you or your child. Contact the centre on 06 867 7565 and we will help.
