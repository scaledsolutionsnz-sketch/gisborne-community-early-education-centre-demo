Days full of learning

Our qualified kaiako follow each child's lead through the first five years, from the nursery through to school. Every day mixes free play, real conversations and time outside.

Under twos

The nursery

A calm space for our youngest tamariki. Each pēpi has their own Tautoko teacher who handles their care routines, so faces stay familiar and days stay settled.

Two to five

Preschool days

All-day or part-day education and care for children aged two to five, with sessions shaped around your whānau routine and the run-up to school.

Free movement

Play their way

We follow the ideas of Emmi Pikler and Magda Gerber: babies and toddlers move, climb and explore at their own pace, without being hurried or propped.

Inquiry learning

Big questions

Children's working theories get taken seriously here. Kaiako help tamariki question, reason and problem-solve, building learning out of their own ideas.

Language

Stories and waiata

Books, songs and unhurried conversation run through every day, growing the language tamariki need long before they pick up a pencil.

Care routines

Kai and rest

Mealtimes and sleep are treated as learning too. Tamariki eat together without being rushed, and rest when their bodies need it.

Community owned

A centre owned by its community

Te Whare Tiaki Tamariki sits on the banks of the Taruheru River in central Gisborne, where spring tides and riverside birdlife set the scene for the day. We are a not-for-profit run for local whānau, and every dollar goes back into the tamariki.

Our team of qualified, experienced early childhood teachers believes genuine relationships come first. Get those right and the learning follows.

  • Community-based and not-for-profit, governed by local families
  • Licensed for 55 children, including a dedicated space for 25 under-twos
  • Long-established Tautoko primary care system, so every child has their person
